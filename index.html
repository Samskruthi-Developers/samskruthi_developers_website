Vivakti

Problem-Solving Operating System

Most people don't need another app to search through. They need to describe a problem in their own words and be handed a way through it. Vivakti reads the problem, works out why it's happening, builds a strategy around it, and stays with you through execution and results.

It isn't a chatbot that answers questions. It's a system that turns a problem into a plan — with objectives, cost estimates, timelines, and a way to track whether it actually worked.
