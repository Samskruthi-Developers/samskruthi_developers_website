About

Samskruthi Developers exists at the intersection of technology, creativity, and practical problem solving.

We build products and services around actual user problems rather than technology for technology's sake. That means we spend as much time understanding the situation as we do building the solution.

The company works across two connected disciplines: engineering software that solves something concrete, and creating original visual design that gives a brand an identity of its own.

  • Problem first

    Every product starts with a problem someone actually has, not with a technology looking for a use.

  • Original by default

    Design work is directed by people. Nothing is assembled from a template that a hundred other brands already used.

  • Useful, then beautiful

    Craft matters — but only after the thing works, ships, and measurably helps the person using it.

02

Graphic Design

The problem

Everyone has access to AI-generated templates. Very few brands have something that actually feels like theirs.

Many businesses now rely on the same generic, AI-generated templates for Instagram posts, advertisements, announcements, and promotional content. The output is fast — and it looks like everyone else's.

As AI-generated content becomes increasingly common, generic visuals are becoming harder to distinguish. Original, brand-specific creative work helps businesses stand apart from template-driven content.

We create original graphics directed by people and tailored to the specific brand, message, audience, and campaign.

Vivakti

An AI-powered problem-solving app.

Vivakti is designed to help users solve real-life problems from start to finish — from the moment a problem is described to the moment its solution is measured.

Type
AI-powered app
Purpose
Real-world problem solving
Approach
Problem → Results → Growth

The concept

  1. Problem
  2. Analysis
  3. Strategy
  4. Execution
  5. Tracking
  6. Results
  7. Growth
01

How Vivakti works

Instead of figuring out which service, expert or solution they need, users simply describe the problem. Vivakti turns it into an actionable plan.

  1. 01
    01

    Describe the problem

    The user explains their problem naturally, in their own words — no forms, no guessing which service or expert they need.

  2. 02
    02

    Understand the root cause

    Vivakti analyses the problem and identifies the underlying causes rather than the visible symptoms.

  3. 03
    03

    Build a strategy

    Vivakti creates a customised strategy based on the user's specific situation.

    • Objectives
    • Estimated costs
    • Timeline
    • Expected outcomes
    • Required actions
  4. 04
    04

    Set up & execute

    The user is guided step by step through implementing the solution.

  5. 05
    05

    Track progress

    Vivakti helps monitor progress in real time as the plan is carried out.

  6. 06
    06

    Measure results

    At the end, Vivakti evaluates whether the solution actually worked.

  7. 07
    07

    Discover what's next

    Vivakti can suggest future opportunities for improvement and growth.

02

In simple words

You describe the problem.
Vivakti figures out what is actually causing it, builds a plan, helps you execute it, tracks the progress, and measures the result.

  • Custom strategy
  • Objectives & required actions
  • Estimated costs & timeline
  • Guided execution
  • Real-time progress tracking
  • Outcome measurement
  • Next-step suggestions

Upasthiti

Attendance tracking made simple.

Upasthiti is an attendance tracking app designed especially for students who struggle to keep track of their attendance.

Type
Student app
Purpose
Attendance tracking
Flow
Timetable → Record → Track

The idea, in three steps

  1. Enter your timetable
  2. Record attendance
  3. Track your percentage
01

How it works

  1. 01
    01

    Enter your timetable

    Students enter their class timetable once, so the app knows which classes happen when.

  2. 02
    02

    Record attendance

    Students mark whether they were present or absent as they attend their classes.

  3. 03
    03

    Track attendance

    Upasthiti calculates and displays the attendance percentage.

  4. 04
    04

    Understand your attendance

    Students can see detailed information about their attendance and keep track of their academic attendance status.

02

Features

  • Attendance Percentage
  • Timetable Management
  • Present/Absent Tracking
  • Subject-wise Attendance
  • Attendance Overview
  • Easy Daily Updates
  • Student-friendly Dashboard
